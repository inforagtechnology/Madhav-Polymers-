About This Range

Glazing and skirting gaskets for glass and panel office-partition systems -- push-fit, bubble and double-glazing seal profiles.

All MP-series profiles are extruded in-house and can be supplied in custom compounds (EPDM / PVC-blend) and colours on request. Roll lengths and box quantities below follow Madhav Polimer's standard packing norms.
