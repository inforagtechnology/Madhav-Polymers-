Our story

Two decades of precision, one standard of trust

Madhav Polymers began as a small tooling workshop in Rajkot and grew into a full-scale sealing solutions manufacturer — without ever loosening our grip on quality.

What drives us

Made to be trusted, not just installed

Every seal we manufacture carries the same promise: it will still be doing its job years after it was fitted. That promise shapes how we compound materials, how we tool our lines, and how we test before anything leaves the factory.

i

Material integrity

Every compound batch is tested for hardness, tensile strength and weather resistance before it reaches the line.

ii

Tooling discipline

CNC-machined dies and fixtures hold tolerances that keep every metre of profile identical to the last.

iii

People & process

A trained floor team following documented SOPs — because consistency is a habit, not an accident.

Milestones

How we got here

Year 1

A workshop in Rajkot

Madhav Polymers opens as a small extrusion workshop, producing basic rubber gaskets for the local fabrication market.

Year 5

Expanding the product line

Weatherstrip and PVC profile seals are added to the catalogue as demand grows beyond Gujarat's borders.

Year 11

ISO 9001 certification

Formal quality management systems are put in place across every stage of production, from raw material to dispatch.

Year 16

In-house tooling & R&D

A dedicated CNC tooling shop and R&D bench let us develop custom profiles in-house rather than outsourcing dies.

Today

A pan-India supplier

Madhav Polymers now serves fabricators, builders and OEMs across India, with export-ready packaging capability.
