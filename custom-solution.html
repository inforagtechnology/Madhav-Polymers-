Bespoke engineering

Don't see your profile? We'll engineer it.

Bring us a drawing, a sample, or just a problem to solve — our in-house tooling and R&D team will take it from brief to production run.

How it works

From brief to production, in four steps

1

Share the brief

Send your drawing, sample, or a description of the application — dimensions, material needs, and the environment it will face.

2

Design & formulate

Our team proposes a profile and compound, balancing flexibility, hardness and weather resistance for your use case.

3

Tool & prototype

We machine a die in-house and run a short sample batch for you to test-fit before committing to volume.

4

Produce & deliver

Once approved, we move to full production with the same inline quality checks used across our standard catalogue.

Where custom helps most

Common custom solution requests

i

Matching a discontinued seal

We reverse-engineer profiles from a physical sample when the original manufacturer is no longer available.

ii

Non-standard channel sizes

When off-the-shelf dimensions don't fit an existing frame, we cut a new die rather than force a compromise.

iii

Specialised compounds

Higher UV resistance, food-grade compliance, or specific colour matching — formulated to your specification sheet.
