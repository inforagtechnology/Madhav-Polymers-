How it works

From brief to production, in four steps

1

Share the brief

Send your drawing, sample, or a description of the application — dimensions, material needs, and the environment it will face.

2

Design & formulate

Our team proposes a profile and compound, balancing flexibility, hardness and weather resistance for your use case.

3

Tool & prototype

We machine a die in-house and run a short sample batch for you to test-fit before committing to volume.

4

Produce & deliver

Once approved, we move to full production with the same inline quality checks used across our standard catalogue.
