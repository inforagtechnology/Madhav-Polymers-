Zero-defect standard

Quality checked at every stage, not just the last

ISO 9001:2015 certified processes govern everything from incoming raw material to the seal that leaves our dispatch bay.

Our process

Five checkpoints, one standard

1

Raw material inspection

Incoming polymer batches are tested for hardness, tensile strength and colour consistency before approval.

2

Compounding

Formulations are mixed to exact ratios and logged, so every batch behaves the same way on the line.

3

Extrusion & tooling

CNC-machined dies hold tolerances to +/- 0.05mm, keeping profile dimensions consistent metre after metre.

4

Inline quality checks

Dimensional and visual inspection happens continuously during the run, not just at the end of it.

5

Packaging & dispatch

Finished seals are packed to prevent transit damage and labelled for full batch traceability.

"Zero-defect manufacturing isn't a slogan here — it's the standard every batch is checked against."
ISO 9001:2015 In-house R&D Export Ready CNC Precision Tooling
Testing & equipment

What we check, and how

i

Hardness & tensile testing

Durometer and tensile-strength testing on every compound batch, logged against a reference standard.

ii

Dimensional inspection

Profile cross-sections are checked against CAD tolerances using calibrated gauges at set line intervals.

iii

Weathering & ageing checks

Accelerated ageing tests confirm seals hold their properties well beyond typical installed lifespans.
